TERMS & CONDITIONS

Booking terms

  • Booths are held for 24 hours from the time of enquiry.
  • The non-refundable deposit must be paid within that 24-hour hold period. If payment is not received, the booking will be cancelled and the booth released.
  • A portion of the booth price is charged as the booking fee. The remaining amount is credited towards your beverage orders.
  • The lead booker must hold a valid MTL Pass or Package. Other guests without one must pay the applicable entry fee at the door.
